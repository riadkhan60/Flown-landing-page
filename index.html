Screen recordings that zoom in where you click.

Flown is a free screen recorder for Windows. Record a demo the way you normally would. Flown zooms in on your clicks, smooths the cursor and sets the video on a clean background before you open the editor.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Open source under the MIT license. Runs on Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11.

Everything is a track you can move.

The editor lays your recording out on a timeline. Flown fills in the zooms when the recording finishes; you decide what stays. Every change can be undone.

  1. Cut, trim and change speed.

    Split at the playhead with S and cut a range with Delete. Any clip can play from 0.25× to 8×. Smart speed finds stretches where nothing moves and nothing is said, then speeds them up or cuts them.

    Clip cut 4×

  2. Zooms come from your clicks.

    Each one starts just before the click, holds, and eases out while the camera follows the cursor. Change the level, draw the exact region to fill the frame, or pull back to show more background. Zooms next to each other pan from one to the next.

    Zoom 2× 1.6× auto 0.8×

  3. Add labels, and show the shortcuts you pressed.

    Press T, type, and drag the text into place on the preview. Shortcuts like Ctrl + C show up as keycaps. Ordinary typing is never recorded.

    Text Click here Ctrl + C

  4. Point at things, and hide what shouldn’t be seen.

    Arrows, boxes, ellipses and a highlighter stay pinned to the content as the camera moves. Blur or pixelate passwords and email addresses, or dim everything outside a spotlight that follows the cursor.

    Shapes Arrow Blur Spotlight

  5. Open with a title, and split long videos into chapters.

    Title cards come centered, big or as a lower third. Add a chapter with M. Chapters are written into the exported file and can be copied as YouTube timestamps.

    Titles Welcome

  6. Captions from what you said.

    Generate them with your own OpenAI or Gemini key, then correct any line on its own track. Choose from five styles, including word-by-word highlighting, or export SRT and VTT files.

    Captions Here’s the dashboard. Click Export. Edit any line.

  7. Put your webcam where it helps.

    A circle, rounded or square bubble with a border and shadow. The background behind you can be blurred, on your PC. Switch layouts as you go: bubble, side by side, large camera or camera only.

    Camera Side by side Large Camera Screen

Set it up once.

Save your recording setup as a preset and your look as the default style. Every new recording starts the way you like it.

Recording

  • A full screen, one window, or an area you drag out
  • Microphone and system audio, kept as separate tracks
  • 30 or 60 fps, with a 3-2-1 countdown
  • Your own hotkeys for stop, pause and “zoom here”
  • Pauses itself when you step away
  • Hides desktop icons while you record
  • Written to disk as you go, so a crash doesn’t lose the take

Cursor

  • Smoothed movement and an adjustable size
  • Keeps the real cursor shape, or switch to a macOS, Windows, white, black or custom cursor
  • Ripple, ring or pulse on every click
  • Optional click sounds
  • Hides when you stop moving it

Style

  • Gradients, solid colours, your own image, 16 wallpapers and moving backgrounds
  • Browser, laptop, monitor and phone frames
  • Padding, corners, glass borders and soft, hard or glow shadows
  • 3D tilt
  • Your logo or a watermark
  • Eight built-in looks, plus your own

Sound

  • Separate volume for your mic and your computer’s sound
  • Noise reduction and even loudness
  • Fade in and out
  • Background music that gets quieter while you talk
  • Remove silences in one step

Export for where it’s going.

Pick a destination and Flown sets the shape, size and frame rate. The recording is reframed to fit, background and all.

  • 16:9 YouTube in 1080p or 4K at 60 fps, X, LinkedIn
  • 9:16 Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
  • 1:1 Square posts
  • Small A GIF for Slack under 15 MB, or email under 25 MB

MP4, MOV, WebM, GIF or audio-only M4A, from 720p to 4K at 30 or 60 fps. Flown encodes on your graphics card when it can (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) and falls back to software when it can’t. Export several sizes in one go, aim for a file size, or copy the finished video to the clipboard and paste it straight into a chat.

Export

Your recordings stay on your PC.

Flown has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It only talks to the internet when you ask it to.

  • Saved where you can find them

    Each recording is a folder in your Videos folder: the video, the cursor data and the project file.

  • Your own AI key, if you want one

    Captions and transcript tools use your OpenAI, Gemini or Claude key. It’s encrypted with your Windows account, and requests go straight to that provider.

  • Background blur runs locally

    The model that separates you from the room behind you ships inside the app, so your camera feed isn’t uploaded anywhere.

  • Shortcuts, never what you type

    Flown notes key combinations like Ctrl + C for the keystroke overlay, and only the timing of typing for zooms. You can turn this off before you record.

Questions

Is Flown free?

Yes. Flown is open source under the MIT license. There’s no account, no subscription and no watermark unless you add your own.

Which versions of Windows does it run on?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and Windows 11. On these, Flown records screens, areas and single windows without the Windows cursor, so it can draw its own smooth one in its place.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the installer?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC” the first time you run it. Select More info, then Run anyway.

Can I record a single window?

Yes. Flown follows the window as it moves or resizes, and keeps auto zoom, click ripples and the smooth cursor working inside it.

What happens if the app crashes while I’m recording?

The recording is written to disk as you go. Anything that didn’t finish shows up under Unfinished recordings on the home screen, where you can recover it.

Is it finished?

Flown is at version 0.1. The Windows capture helper and graphics-card encoding are the newest parts and still need testing on more PCs. If either one fails, Flown falls back to standard capture and software encoding. If something breaks on your machine, open an issue with your Windows version and graphics card.
